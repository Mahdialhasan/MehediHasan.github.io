About me

I am a geospatial researcher and MSc candidate in Applied Geoinformatics at Paris Lodron University of Salzburg, Austria. My research specializes in multi-hazard risk assessment, vulnerability analysis, and disaster risk reduction using GIS, remote sensing, Python, and machine learning.

I leverage high-resolution satellite imagery, spatial statistics, machine learning models, and hydro-climatic data to understand environmental risk dynamics, flood susceptibility, and seismic vulnerability to support resilient communities.

I have authored and co-authored peer-reviewed research papers in Taylor & Francis, Elsevier, and Springer Nature journals focusing on multi-hazard mapping, wetland dynamics, and flash flood prediction[cite: 1, 2]. I also bring hands-on professional industry experience as a GIS Analyst at the Institute of Water Modelling (IWM)[cite: 3].
