About me

I am a geospatial researcher and MSc student in Applied Geoinformatics at Paris Lodron University of Salzburg, Austria. My research examines multi-hazard risk, seismic vulnerability, and flood susceptibility in coastal and riverine environments, with a focus on disaster risk reduction and resilient communities.

I use GIS, remote sensing, and Python, together with high-resolution satellite imagery, spatial statistics, machine learning, hydro-climatic data, and spatial MCDA to understand environmental risk dynamics and how they affect vulnerable populations.

I have authored/co-authored 5 peer-reviewed papers in Taylor & Francis, Elsevier, and Springer Nature journals on multi-hazard mapping, wetland dynamics, and flash flood prediction, with another paper under review on flood susceptibility under hydro-climatic extremes.

I bring four professional roles in GIS and research, most recently as a GIS Analyst at the Institute of Water Modelling (IWM), and I have presented at international conferences in Bangladesh and Thailand, including ICLESSD-2025 and IWTACC-2025.

Beyond research: Having started my academic journey in Bangladesh, I enjoy mentoring students who face academic or research challenges in GIScience and Remote Sensing.
