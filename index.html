About Me

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Applied Geoinformatics at Paris Lodron Universität Salzburg, Austria. My research interests focus on applying geospatial technologies, remote sensing, and GeoAI to assess environmental and disaster risks.

My academic work combines GIS-based spatial modeling, machine learning algorithms, and satellite remote sensing to solve complex spatial problems and support risk-informed decision-making.

Research Interests

GIS & Remote Sensing

Geospatial data analysis, satellite remote sensing, spatial modeling, and environmental mapping.

GeoAI & Machine Learning

Machine learning and deep learning approaches for geospatial applications and spatial predictions.

Environmental Risk

Spatial assessment of climate hazards, natural disaster vulnerability, and environmental resilience.

Selected Projects

Coastal Multi-Hazard Risk Assessment

GIS / Spatial Modeling

GIS-based assessment of flood, cyclone, salinity, and erosion risks with vulnerability analysis and multi-criteria decision-making frameworks.

Urban Thermal Environment Analysis

Remote Sensing / Python

Analysis of land surface temperature, urban environmental changes, and thermal conditions using multi-spectral remote sensing datasets.

Earthquake Risk Assessment

AHP / Spatial Analysis

Spatial assessment of seismic risk and vulnerability using GIS, Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP), and geospatial hazard modeling.
